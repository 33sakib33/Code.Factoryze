A workbench that shows the game you are playing

Factoryze is a coding test with a budget: fix the task, spend as few tokens as you can. Today the app looks like an editor with a grey bar on top. This proposal keeps the layout, colours and typeface, and makes three things visible: the code, the tests, and the tokens you are spending against the mark to beat.

During an attempt

The editor is most of the screen, so coloured code does more for the look than any other change. The gauge at the top is the one new idea: teal is what you spent, one block per prompt, and the amber tick is what the reference AI spent on the same task.
